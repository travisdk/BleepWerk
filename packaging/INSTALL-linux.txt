BleepForge – installation on Linux
==================================

The archive contains BleepForge in two plug-in formats, CLAP and VST®3.
Install the one your DAW uses, or both.

CLAP (Bitwig Studio, REAPER and others):

    mkdir -p ~/.clap
    cp BleepForge.clap ~/.clap/

VST3:

    mkdir -p ~/.vst3
    cp -r BleepForge.vst3 ~/.vst3/

(Use /usr/lib/clap/ and /usr/lib/vst3/ instead to install for all users.)

Restart your DAW or rescan plug-ins. BleepForge appears as an instrument.
